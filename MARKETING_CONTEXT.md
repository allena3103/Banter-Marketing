# Banter — Marketing Context

Background on Banter for anyone doing its marketing (content, copy, social, app store). It covers what the app is, who it's for, and how it's being promoted so far.

## What Banter is

Banter is a mobile app (iOS and Android) for live video debates. You pick a side on a topic, get matched with a real person who disagrees, and argue it out face to face, live. You can also host or join live debate rooms with a group, chat, follow people and message them.

**One-liner:** Debate strangers live on any topic.

## Why it exists

Banter gives people a place to say what they think out loud, live, instead of typing it into a comment section.

* **Speak your mind live.** Say your opinion out loud, in real time, to a real person.
* **Talk to people who disagree.** The matchmaking pairs you with someone on the opposite side, so every conversation is a real exchange, not an echo chamber.
* **Keep it constructive.** The goal is a real back-and-forth about the topic, not a pile-on.
* **Make it fun.** Debating should feel like a game. Timers, soundboard effects, voice messages and hot-take prompts keep it light.
* **Learn something.** Hearing the other side from a real person teaches you more than reading about it. You may change your mind, or come away with a sharper argument.
* **Challenge people.** Test your opinions against others in a low-stakes, entertaining setting.

## Who it's for

* **18+ only.** The app enforces this at signup.
* People who like hot takes, arguing for fun, debate, politics, philosophy, sports takes, pop culture and current events.
* People who enjoy "talk to strangers" or live video formats, and the audience of debate and commentary creators.
* Swearing is allowed, slurs are blocked, and users can report, block and hide content.

## Core features (in user terms)

1. **VS mode (the main feature).** The app shows a debate prompt (e.g. "College is not worth the debt"). You tap **Agree** or **Disagree**, get matched with someone who picked the other side, and debate them 1:1 on split-screen video. Free debates have a 7-minute timer.
2. **Live rooms.** Go live and host a debate room. Viewers watch and chat, and they can raise a hand to join the stage (up to 6 speakers, or 10 with Premium). Rooms can be public or private.
3. **Home feed.** Swipe through debates that are live right now.
4. **Fun extras.** Text-to-voice messages with soundboard effects (users earn free voice credits by watching ads), plus a shared whiteboard in VS debates.
5. **Social.** Follow people, DM them, and get notified when hosts you follow go live.

## Premium

A subscription through the App Store or Google Play. It adds:

* Unlimited VS debate time (no 7-minute cap)
* Your own custom debate prompts
* Drawing on the VS whiteboard
* 10-speaker stages in live rooms
* Priority when raising your hand in rooms
* A premium badge and premium soundboard effects

**Referral codes** give new users a free **7-day Premium trial**. A separate code per channel, campaign or video format makes it possible to track which one drives installs.

Prices aren't listed here. Ask the owner, and don't invent prices.

## Brand voice

* Bold, playful and confident, with a little competitive edge ("think you're right? prove it").
* Fun first, then thoughtful. It's for arguing *with* people, not *at* them.
* Avoid: rage-bait framing, attacking groups of people, or presenting Banter as a place for hate or harassment.

## Positioning keywords

debate app, live debate, hot takes, talk to strangers, versus / VS, challenge, opponent, argue, disagree, opinions, current events, video chat, meet new people

## Example prompt topics

School and career ("College is not worth the debt"), tech ("AI will take more jobs than it creates"), society and politics ("Billionaires should not exist"), environment ("Nuclear energy is the future"), lifestyle and pop culture. Prompts are worded as one-sided statements, so users simply agree or disagree.
