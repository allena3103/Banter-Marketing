# Hook angles for Banter

Each spec has a `hook_angle`. Track results per angle and double down on winners (the playbook says to judge after about 50 posts).

| Angle | Pattern | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| controversial | Opinions that start fights | "5 opinions that will start a fight at Thanksgiving dinner" · "Opinions that get you kicked out of the group chat" |
| surprise | What people secretly think | "Things most people secretly agree with but won't say out loud" · "Hot takes that are actually correct" |
| insider | Lessons from debating live | "I debated 50 strangers live. Here's what actually wins an argument" |
| challenge | Dares the viewer to pick a side | "Agree or disagree? Most people get #4 wrong" |
| value | Practical debate and communication tips | "7 ways to win any argument without yelling" |

Rules:
- Hook text is 90 characters or less, with one highlighted phrase.
- Open a curiosity gap so people swipe.
- Edgy is fine, hateful isn't (see the brand voice). Never target a group of people.
- Insider hooks must be true. Don't claim numbers ("50 strangers") the owner hasn't confirmed.
