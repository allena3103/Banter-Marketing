# Debate prompt bank

One-sided statements for body slides, 110 characters or less. Add prompts that do well in the app or in research.

## Money and work
- Tipping culture is a scam.
- College is not worth the debt.
- Billionaires should not exist.
- Most meetings could have been an email.
- A 4-day work week should be the law.

## Tech
- AI will take more jobs than it creates.
- Kids shouldn't have smartphones before 16.
- Social media has done more harm than good.

## Society and environment
- Nuclear energy is the future.
- Voting should be mandatory.

## Lifestyle and pop culture
- Being early is just being on time with extra steps.
- Group projects teach you nothing except who to avoid.
- Pineapple belongs on pizza.
- The book is always better than the movie.
